## QC summary

FastQC results for all 32 samples were good overall. Per-base quality was high throughout. The "Per Base Sequence Content" failures were confined to the first ~12 bases of every sample, which is the expected random-hexamer priming bias of RNA-seq libraries. Duplication warnings were in the range typical for RNA-seq (39–56%). Overrepresented sequences were low-level TruSeq adapter dimers (≤1.5% of reads).

At the read-assignment step (featureCounts), a median of 60.5% of records were assigned to genes (range 34.6–66.5%), with multimapping reads as the main unassigned category (median 27.5%). Note that featureCounts counts multimapping reads once per alignment, so these percentages slightly understate the per-read assignment rate. Two samples were examined in more detail:

1. **ERR2598151 (Brain, 20w, Male)** had the lowest sequencing depth (16.8M reads) and the highest duplication (64%). Only ~18% of its reads were unique in FastQC's estimate, against 32–49% in the other samples, which indicates reduced library complexity (likely low input or PCR over-amplification) rather than a single contaminating sequence. GC content was normal. At the featureCounts stage it was typical: 12.3M reads assigned to genes (~73% of raw reads; 61% of featureCounts records), with multimapping (27% of records) at the dataset median. Its effective depth is lower than the raw read count suggests, but it still has ample assigned reads.

2. **ERR2598121 (Forebrain, 16w, Male)** was sequenced deeply (29.4M reads) but had the lowest assignment rate (39% of records), with most of the remainder multimapping (~54%). No single sequence was overrepresented. However, it had the largest share of reads at very high copy number (>1,000 copies), which is consistent with abundant multi-copy transcripts such as rRNA or mitochondrial RNA. These reads fragment into many distinct sequences and so are not flagged by FastQC's overrepresented-sequences check. Because multimapping reads are excluded rather than misassigned, they reduce usable depth but do not distort gene counts. The sample still retained roughly 11M assigned reads.

Both samples were retained for analysis without deduplication. Their inclusion is to be confirmed by checking that they cluster with their biological group on a PCA of variance-stabilised counts, and if not, by testing whether results change when they are excluded.
